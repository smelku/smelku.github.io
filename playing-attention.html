playing attention

These images were captured on a Google Pixel 2XL using its Sony Exmor IMX362 camera module and the first implementation of Google's Pixel Visual Core image processor.

The Pixel 2XL was a unique flagship phone at the time, with Google choosing to ship with a single camera and achieve features like portrait mode's depth of field, or the up to 8x zoom, completely digitally, while other contemporary flagships (iPhone X and Galaxy Note 8) opted for two rear camera lenses: an f/1.7 wide-angle and f/2.4 telephoto for 2x optical zoom.

Un-zoomed, the Pixel 2XL's camera is sharp and expressive, capable of good color accuracy and dynamic range. Zooming anwhere past 2x, though, causes extreme amounts of filtering and digital smoothing. I fell in love with the aggressive levels of creamy blur and post-processing meant to hide the digital noise, and the final images often had a dreamy oil painting quality to them.

playing attention is the result of my emotional and expressive zooming in, compartmentalizing out of necessity as I navigated a difficult and uncertain transitory period of my life and used unhealthy coping mechanisms. I took solace in retreating into these little painted postage stamps anywhere I went. All images were shot between 2017–2024 and are unedited SOOC JPGs, with the exception of occasional cropping.
